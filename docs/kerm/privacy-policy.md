# Privacy Policy for Kerm

**Effective date:** September 22, 2026

Kerm ("the App") is a productivity application developed and published by **spastry**.

This Privacy Policy explains how Kerm handles information when you use the App.

## 1. Information We Collect

Kerm does not collect, transmit, or sell personal information.

Information that you enter into Kerm, such as goals, tasks, milestones, and progress data, is stored locally on your device.

Kerm does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

## 2. How Your Information Is Used

The information stored in Kerm is used solely to provide the App's functionality, including:

* Creating and managing goals
* Creating and completing daily tasks
* Tracking progress
* Maintaining your preferences and app state

This information is processed locally on your device.

## 3. Data Sharing

Kerm does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Kerm does not transmit your locally stored productivity data to external servers.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Kerm stores app data locally on your device.

Because the data is stored on your device, its security also depends on the security of your device and operating system.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

Kerm does not maintain a separate server-side copy of your data.

You can delete locally stored Kerm data by using the appropriate data-clearing or uninstall options provided by your device.

Uninstalling Kerm may remove locally stored app data, depending on your device and operating system settings.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Kerm does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because Kerm does not require users to provide personal information or create an account, it does not intentionally collect children's personal information.

## 7. Third-Party Services

Kerm does not currently use third-party services that collect personal information from users.

If this changes in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Kerm or its data practices.

Any updated version will be made available at the same location as this Privacy Policy, with the effective date updated accordingly.
